    same air

    Drawing on oral history interviews with Goodman Arts Centre's custodians, same air disperses their recollections of routine, friendship and care into an online listening space. Their voices are broken into grains of sound and surface only briefly, as they might in passing along a corridor. The work also responds to Singapore's live air-quality readings: as the haze thickens, the images of the custodians become increasingly obscured, reflecting how their presence and labour often go unseen.

    Made in October 2026, as smoke from forest and peatland fires in Indonesia drifted across the region, bringing Singapore its most severe transboundary haze since 2015.

    A companion to Home(work) by a.syadiq, at Blk G and Blk B on 24 Oct.
